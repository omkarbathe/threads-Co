Measuring Protocol

Refer to the diagram for precise
anatomical measurement points.

01. Chest / Bust

Measure around the fullest part of the chest, keeping the tape level under the arms.

02. Natural Waist

Measure at the narrowest part of the torso, typically above the belly button.

03. Hip

Standing with heels together, measure around the fullest part of the hips.
