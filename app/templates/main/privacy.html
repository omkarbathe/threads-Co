01. Information Collection

Threads & Co. collects personal data necessary to provide our bespoke services. This includes identifiers such as your name, contact information, and shipping address provided during checkout or account creation.

We also collect transactional information and browsing data to refine your experience within our digital atelier.

02. Usage of Data

Your information is utilized solely for:

03. Data Protection

We implement rigorous security protocols to ensure the integrity of your personal information. We do not sell or trade your data to third-party entities. All financial transactions are processed through encrypted, industry-standard gateways.

04. Your Rights

You maintain the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. For inquiries regarding your privacy or to exercise your rights, please contact our privacy officer at privacy@threadsco.com.

Commitment to transparency and discretion is the foundation of our client relationships.
